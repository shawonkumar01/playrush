PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Deployed at: https://playrush-peach.vercel.app/

1. Introduction & Commitment to Player Privacy

At PlayRush ("we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that is gathered and recorded when you visit our website at https://playrush-peach.vercel.app/, as well as how that information is utilized in accordance with global data protection regulations and advertising network standards.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike conventional gaming platforms, PlayRush is engineered for barrier-free instant play:

  • We do not require account registration, usernames, or passwords.
  • We do not collect credit card numbers, billing information, or financial details.
  • We do not track, buy, or sell personally identifiable information (PII).

3. Use of Browser LocalStorage

To maintain high scores and game progression without requiring remote user profiles or backend databases, PlayRush utilizes your web browser's standard localStorage API. The following keys are stored exclusively on your local device:

  • playrush_neon_dodge_highscore: Stores your personal record score in Neon Dodge so you can measure improvement across runs.
  • playrush_total_orbs: Records the cumulative number of energy orbs gathered across runs for daily challenge quest tracking.
  • playrush_best_survival_time: Stores your longest continuous survival time in seconds to verify challenge completion.
  • playrush_sound_muted: Remembers your audio synthesizer preference (muted vs. active sound).

Player Control: This data never leaves your device. You can clear or reset this data at any time through your browser's Developer Tools or by clearing site data in your browser privacy settings.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Compliance

PlayRush is supported by digital advertising to keep all arcade titles free for all players. We partner with reputable third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense.

In accordance with Google's advertising policies and industry transparency standards:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized or contextual advertisements to our users based on their browsing activity across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's official Ad Settings page at https://www.google.com/settings/ads.
  • Alternatively, you may opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for interest-based advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page at www.aboutads.info/choices/.

5. Web Beacons & Standard Server Logs

Like most modern websites deployed on content delivery networks (including Vercel), server log files are generated automatically. These files log visitors when they access pages. Information gathered may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized/truncated where required by law)
  • Browser user-agent and operating system
  • Referring / exit pages and date/time stamps
  • Number of clicks to analyze trends and monitor infrastructure security against DDoS attacks

This information is not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of what personal data a business collects. PlayRush collects zero personal identity data.
  • Right to Delete: You can delete all locally stored gameplay metrics at any time by clearing your browser cache.
  • Right to Opt-Out: PlayRush does not sell or share personal information as defined under the CCPA.

7. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EEA/UK Players

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom:

  • The legal basis for processing server log data is legitimate interest in maintaining site security, uptime, and delivering requested game assets.
  • Where consent is required for non-essential cookies (such as personalized advertising cookies), users are provided options through standard consent management frameworks.
  • You retain the right to request access, rectification, erasure, or restriction of processing of any personal information we may hold.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting the safety of children online is critically important. PlayRush offers casual arcade games suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If a parent or guardian believes that PlayRush has collected personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly take appropriate action.

9. External Links & Third-Party Sites

Our portal may contain links to external sites (such as our community Discord or GitHub repositories). Please be aware that we have no control over the content and practices of these external sites and cannot accept responsibility or liability for their respective privacy policies.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in regulatory standards, ad serving guidelines, or platform enhancements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
