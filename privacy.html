PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 16, 2026 • Deployed at: https://playrush-peach.vercel.app/

1. Overview & Scope

At PlayRush ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible at https://playrush-peach.vercel.app/, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we process when you visit our website, play our web-based arcade games, or communicate with us through our contact form.

PlayRush is designed for direct browser play without mandatory account registration, logins, or payment transactions.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We collect information only when you choose to provide it directly to us or through automated technical interactions necessary to serve web content:

A. Contact Form Communications

When you voluntarily submit an inquiry, bug report, or message via our contact form on the About page, we collect the following details:

  • Name: The name you enter to identify yourself.
  • Email Address: Used solely to reply to your inquiry.
  • Subject: The topic category you select (e.g., General Feedback, Bug Report, Business Inquiry).
  • Message Content: The text details of your message, suggestion, or question.

Purpose of Processing: We process this information exclusively to review your communication, respond to your inquiries, investigate reported game issues, and provide technical support. We do not use contact form submissions for unsolicited marketing lists or sell this information to third parties.

Third-Party Form Processor (Formspree): Our contact form is powered by Formspree (Formspree, Inc.). When you submit the form, your entries are securely routed through Formspree's API and forwarded directly to our team inbox at contact.playrush@gmail.com. Formspree acts as a service provider processing these form transmissions on our behalf. For details on how Formspree processes and protects data, please review the Formspree Privacy Policy.

B. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require user registration, passwords, or personal profile creation to access our games.
  • We do not collect credit card numbers, banking details, or financial payment data.
  • We do not collect precise GPS geolocation data.

3. Browser LocalStorage (Game State Persistence)

To maintain gameplay metrics and high scores locally without transmitting data to remote servers or requiring user accounts, PlayRush uses your web browser's built-in localStorage API.

The following specific keys are stored on your local device:

  • playrush_neon_dodge_highscore: Stores your highest numeric score achieved in Neon Dodge so you can track personal records.
  • playrush_total_orbs: Records the cumulative number of energy orbs collected across game runs for daily challenge quest progress.
  • playrush_best_survival_time: Records your longest continuous survival time (in seconds) in a single run of Neon Dodge to verify quest completion.
  • playrush_sound_muted: Stores your audio toggle preference ("true" or "false") so the sound synthesizer respects your choice on return visits.

Data Retention and User Control: These keys exist solely on your local device and are not automatically transmitted to our servers. You can inspect, modify, or delete them at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings or executing localStorage.clear() in your browser Developer Tools console.

4. Web Hosting & Server Diagnostics (Vercel)

PlayRush is deployed on Vercel (Vercel Inc.). When you request pages or assets from https://playrush-peach.vercel.app/, Vercel's edge network and hosting infrastructure may automatically process standard HTTP request logs.

These technical server logs may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser user-agent string and operating system information
  • Referring URLs, requested resource paths, and timestamps
  • HTTP status codes and data transfer metrics

This technical data is processed by the hosting provider for core infrastructure operations, including delivering requested web assets, traffic routing, performance monitoring, and defending against malicious cyber activity (such as Distributed Denial of Service attacks). PlayRush does not maintain a private server database linking these logs to identified individuals. For information regarding Vercel's infrastructure practices, please consult the Vercel Privacy Policy.
