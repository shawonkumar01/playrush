PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 16, 2026 • Deployed at: https://playrush-peach.vercel.app/

1. Overview & Scope

At PlayRush ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible at https://playrush-peach.vercel.app/, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we process when you visit our website, play our web-based arcade games, or communicate with us through our contact form.

PlayRush is designed for direct browser play without mandatory account registration, logins, or payment transactions.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We collect information only when you choose to provide it directly to us or through automated technical interactions necessary to serve web content:

A. Contact Form Communications

When you voluntarily submit an inquiry, bug report, or message via our contact form on the About page, we collect the following details:

  • Name: The name you enter to identify yourself.
  • Email Address: Used to communicate with you and reply to your inquiry.
  • Subject: The topic category you select (such as General Feedback, Bug Report, or Business Inquiry).
  • Message Content: The text details of your message, suggestion, or question.

Purpose of Processing: We process this information to review your communication, respond to your inquiries, investigate reported game issues, and provide player support. We do not sell this contact information or use it for unsolicited marketing lists.

Third-Party Form Processor (Formspree): Our contact form utilizes Formspree (Formspree, Inc.) as a third-party service provider to process and transmit form submissions to our inbox at contact.playrush@gmail.com. Formspree handles this data in accordance with their service terms and privacy practices. For details regarding their processing and handling of submitted information, please review the Formspree Privacy Policy.

B. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require user registration, passwords, or personal profile creation to access our games.
  • We do not collect credit card numbers, banking details, or financial payment data.
  • We do not collect precise GPS geolocation data.

3. Browser LocalStorage (Game State Persistence)

To maintain gameplay metrics and high scores across sessions without requiring player accounts or server-side user databases, PlayRush uses your web browser's built-in localStorage API.

The following specific keys are stored locally on your device:

  • playrush_neon_dodge_highscore: Stores your highest numeric score achieved in Neon Dodge for personal record tracking.
  • playrush_total_orbs: Records the cumulative number of energy orbs collected across game runs for daily challenge quest progress.
  • playrush_best_survival_time: Records your longest continuous survival time (in seconds) in a single run of Neon Dodge to verify quest completion.
  • playrush_sound_muted: Stores your audio toggle preference ("true" or "false") so the sound synthesizer respects your choice on return visits.

Data Storage and User Control: These values are stored locally within your browser and are not transmitted to PlayRush servers. You can clear or reset these values at any time through your browser's site data settings or developer tools.

4. Web Hosting & Server Logs (Vercel)

PlayRush is hosted on infrastructure provided by Vercel Inc. When you access https://playrush-peach.vercel.app/, Vercel's edge network and hosting systems may automatically process standard technical request information to deliver website content and maintain network security.

This technical data processed by Vercel may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser user-agent strings and operating system details
  • Referring URLs, requested resource paths, and access timestamps
  • HTTP status codes and data transfer volume

This network data is processed by Vercel as our hosting provider for infrastructure operations, routing, performance monitoring, and service reliability. PlayRush does not directly operate a separate logging database to track individual visitors. For information on how Vercel handles server and network data, please refer to the Vercel Privacy Policy.
