PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

At PlayRush, privacy is fundamentally embedded into our technical architecture. We do not require account registration, passwords, credit card details, or sensitive personal data to play our games.

2. Use of Browser LocalStorage

To deliver a persistent arcade experience without sending your gameplay data to remote servers, we store game state directly on your own device using standard browser localStorage APIs:

  • playrush_neon_dodge_highscore: Stores your personal record score so you can track improvements.
  • playrush_total_orbs: Tracks cumulative orbs collected for daily challenges.
  • playrush_sound_muted: Remembers your audio preferences (muted or active sound).

This data remains exclusively on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

3. Advertising & Cookies

PlayRush is funded through display advertising to remain 100% free for everyone. Third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or adjusting your Google Ad Settings. We strictly forbid misleading, intrusive, or pop-under advertising units.

4. Terms of Service & Fair Play

All games and media assets on PlayRush are provided "as-is" for personal entertainment. Users agree not to reverse-engineer malicious exploits or disrupt services.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Our browser arcade games are family-friendly and accessible without personal data entry.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our privacy officer at privacy@playrush.games.
