HOW TO PLAY

Quick guide to master Neon Dodge and dominate the high score table.

1

Control Your Ship

Use Arrow Keys or W, A, S, D on desktop. On mobile or tablets, simply touch and drag directly across the screen.

2

Dodge Hazards

Evade red lasers, rotating polygonal asteroids, and tracking mines. One single collision without a shield ends your run!

3

Collect Orbs

Grab Cyan Orbs (+50 pts), Gold Orbs (2X multiplier boost!), and rare Emerald Shields for 1-hit invulnerability.

4

Climb Ranks

Points accumulate every second you survive plus bonus orbs. Your high score persists securely in your browser's local storage.
