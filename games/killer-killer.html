How to Play Killer Killer

Overview & Pass-and-Play

Killer Killer is a local party game played on one shared device by 4 to 12 players. Players take turns holding the device during secret night phases and voting rounds.

The Roles

  • Killer (1): Eliminates citizens secretly at night. Wins when killers equal or outnumber non-killers.
  • Doctor (1): Secretly protects one player each night from the Killer. Can protect themselves.
  • Police (1): Investigates one player each night to uncover whether they are the Killer.
  • Citizens: Listen to word clues, spot inconsistencies, debate, and vote to eliminate the Killer.

The Secret Word Challenge

Every match, a random category and secret word are chosen.

  • Citizens, Doctor & Police receive the actual secret word (e.g. "MANGO").
  • The Killer receives ONLY the category (e.g. "FRUITS") and does NOT know the exact word!
  • During daytime, each player gives ONE verbal clue out loud. The Killer must bluff their way through!
  • The Killer has one private chance to guess the actual word for a bonus point.

Night & Voting Sequence

  • Night: Killer attacks, Doctor protects, Police investigates. If the Doctor's target matches the Killer's target, nobody dies!
  • Day: Night outcome is announced publicly. Word clues are given, followed by open debate and nominations.
  • Secret Voting: Every living player casts a private secret vote on the shared device. Most votes eliminates that suspect! Ties result in no elimination.
